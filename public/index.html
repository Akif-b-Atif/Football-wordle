How to play

Find the hidden footballer in 8 guesses. Every guess shows a row of clues telling you how close you are.

The colours

  • Green — exact match
  • Yellow — close, but not exact
  • Gray — not close

What counts as “close”?

ClueYellow when…
NationDifferent country, same continente.g. you guess Portugal, answer is England
ClubDifferent club, same leaguee.g. you guess Arsenal, answer is Chelsea
PosDifferent position, same group (attackers, midfielders or defenders)e.g. LW and ST
AgeWithin 2 years
OVRWithin 2 rating points
Skill MovesWithin 1 star is yellow; arrows show whether the answer has more or fewer stars
Weak FootWithin 1 star is yellow; arrows show whether the answer has more or fewer stars
AttributesPACE, SHOOTING, PASSING, DRIBBLING, DEFENDING and PHYSICALITY are each within 3 rating points

Arrows

▲ means the answer's number is higher than your guess; ▼ means lower. You'll see them on Age, OVR, Skill Moves, Weak Foot and the six player attributes, whatever the colour.

Player attributes

Tap Stats inside the OVR tile to expand the six attribute clues: Pace (PAC), Shooting (SHO), Passing (PAS), Dribbling (DRI), Defending (DEF) and Physicality (PHY). They start collapsed to keep each guess easy to scan.

Shared PlayStyles

Only PlayStyles your guess has in common with the answer are shown.

Good to know

  • The hidden player is chosen from the 200 highest-rated players. You can guess any player in the men's dataset, across its leagues.
  • Daily Challenge gives everyone the same footballer each day. Unlimited mode picks a fresh random player every game.

Dataset: EA SPORTS FC 27 men's player data, snapshot from 12 September 2026. Player clubs and ratings may change after this snapshot.
