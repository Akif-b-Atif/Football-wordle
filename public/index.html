How to play

Find the hidden footballer in 8 guesses. Every guess shows a row of clues telling you how close you are.

The colours

  • Green — exact match
  • Yellow — close, but not exact
  • Gray — not close

What counts as “close”?

ClueYellow when…
NationDifferent country, same continente.g. you guess Portugal, answer is England
ClubDifferent club, same leaguee.g. you guess Arsenal, answer is Chelsea
PosDifferent position, same group (attackers, midfielders or defenders)e.g. LW and ST
AgeWithin 2 years
OVRWithin 2 rating points
HeightWithin 3 cm

Arrows

▲ means the answer's number is higher than your guess; ▼ means lower. You'll see them on Age, OVR and Height, whatever the colour.

Shared traits

Playing-style traits (like Flair or Power Header) that your guess has in common with the answer. Only shared ones are shown.

Example

The answer is Harry Kane (England, Tottenham). You guess Raheem Sterling:

NationEngland
ClubMan City
PosLW
Age26
OVR88
Height170
  • Same country → green.
  • Different club, both in the Premier League → yellow. Both are attackers → yellow.
  • Age and OVR are within 2 of the answer, and ▲ says the answer is a bit higher → yellow.
  • 170 cm is more than 3 cm off, so it's gray; ▲ says the answer is taller.

Good to know

  • The hidden player is always a well-known star (one of the ~180 highest-rated players), but you can guess anyone from the Big 5 leagues.
  • Daily Challenge gives everyone the same footballer each day. Unlimited mode picks a fresh random player every game.

Heads up: all player info (clubs, ages, ratings and heights) is as of 2022 (FIFA 22 data). Players may have since moved, retired or changed rating.
