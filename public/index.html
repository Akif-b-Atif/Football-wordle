How to play

Guess the hidden footballer in 8 tries. After each guess, the row tells you how close you are.

  • Green — exact match
  • Yellow — position only: same positional group, not exact spot
  • Gray — no match
  • ↑ / ↓ Arrows — for Age / Overall / Weak Foot / Skill Moves: the hidden player's value is higher or lower than your guess
  • ✓ Shared Traits — any gameplay traits your guess has in common with the hidden player

Daily Challenge gives everyone the same footballer each day. Unlimited mode picks a fresh random player every game.
